# IELTS AI Coach — Launch Strategy

**Role:** Founder & Chief Product Officer
**Document:** Go-To-Market & Launch Playbook
**Status:** Final — pre-launch execution plan

---

## 0. Executive Summary

**We are not monetizing yet.** This launch exists for one reason: to make IELTS AI Coach the **best FREE AI-powered IELTS preparation platform in the world** — and in doing so, build a massive, engaged, loyal user base before we ever ask anyone for money.

The strategy is simple and disciplined:

1. **Launch a genuinely world-class free product** — not a "freemium teaser," but the full mentorship experience: diagnostic, personalized roadmap, daily missions, AI writing/speaking feedback, progress analytics, and a real community.
2. **Remove every barrier to entry** — no credit card, no paywall, no feature-gating of core value. The product *is* the marketing.
3. **Become a feedback engine** — every user action, bug report, and feature request feeds a weekly iteration loop. We ship, measure, learn, repeat.
4. **Build the community as a moat** — a thriving community of IELTS candidates who recommend us, defend us, and contribute content becomes the strongest acquisition channel and the strongest defense against competitors.
5. **Only consider monetization after we've proven retention** — the data (activation, 30-day retention, band improvement, referral rate) will tell us when and how to monetize. That decision is deliberately deferred.

**North Star:** *Number of students who reach their target band using IELTS AI Coach.*

## 1. Product Context & Launch Philosophy

### 1.1 What We Are Launching

IELTS AI Coach is an **AI Personal IELTS Mentor** — not another IELTS app. It:

- Assesses the student's baseline with a **20-minute diagnostic** (writing, speaking, vocabulary).
- Generates a **personalized, adaptive roadmap** from diagnostic → target band → exam date.
- Schedules **daily missions** that auto-shift when life happens (the Adaptive Scheduler).
- Provides **AI writing assessment** and **AI speaking practice** with instant band scores and actionable feedback.
- Tracks **progress, streaks, predicted band, and readiness** through an analytics dashboard.
- Recommends **100% free, high-quality resources** from trusted providers.

### 1.2 Launch Philosophy (The Rules)

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Free means FREE.** The full core loop is unlocked for everyone during launch. | Trust is the currency of this market. Students have been burned by "free trials." |
| 2 | **Quality over quantity.** We'd rather have 1,000 students who love us than 100,000 who churn. | Retention is the real growth engine. A loved product compounds. |
| 3 | **Speed of iteration is the advantage.** Weekly ship cycles based on real feedback. | Large coaching institutes move slowly. Our ability to listen and improve weekly is our unfair advantage. |
| 4 | **Pedagogy before features.** We never ship gamification or features that inflate band scores or mislead students. | Credibility is everything in education. One dishonest feature destroys years of trust. |
| 5 | **The community is part of the product.** Every user is a contributor — feedback, resources, encouragement. | Coaching institutes sell access to a teacher. We give every student a mentor *and* a peer community. |

### 1.3 Target Launch Profile

| Attribute | Target |
|---|---|
| **Audience** | IELTS candidates worldwide; primary markets: India, Vietnam, China, Middle East, Latin America, Nigeria |
| **Core persona** | Self-studying student (18–35) who cannot afford $300–$2,000 coaching, has 30–90 days before exam |
| **Device mix** | ~70% mobile, ~25% desktop, ~5% tablet |
| **Language** | English-first product; marketing in English + Hindi + Vietnamese + Spanish |
| **Exam module** | Academic (majority) + General Training |
| **Exam date** | Target: first 1,000 active students within 60 days of open beta |

## 2. Beta Launch Strategy

### 2.1 Launch Phases

```
Phase 0:  Private Alpha (Internal)        Week -6 to -4    ~25 internal testers
Phase 1:  Closed Beta (Waitlist)          Week -4 to 0     ~100–300 hand-picked users
Phase 2:  Open Beta (Public Launch)       Week 0+          Everyone — "Best Free IELTS AI"
Phase 3:  Stabilization & Iteration       Week 2–12        Weekly feedback-driven releases
Phase 4:  Growth & Community              Month 3–6        Referrals, content, ambassadors
```

### 2.2 Phase 0 — Private Alpha (Internal)

**Goal:** Prove the core loop works end-to-end without embarrassing bugs.

| Activity | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Testers** | Founders, engineering team, 3–5 trusted IELTS tutors/instructors (paid beta advisors) |
| **Scope** | Full user journey: signup → onboarding → diagnostic → roadmap → dashboard → daily mission → analytics |
| **Key checks** | AI assessment accuracy vs. human tutor, scheduler correctness, streak integrity, data safety |
| **Exit criteria** | Core journey completion ≥ 95% without a blocking bug; AI feedback quality rated ≥ 4/5 by tutors |

### 2.3 Phase 1 — Closed Beta (Waitlist)

**Goal:** Controlled launch with a small, forgiving, high-signal user group.

| Activity | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Recruitment** | Landing page waitlist; recruit from Reddit (r/IELTS), Facebook IELTS groups, university student clubs, Telegram groups |
| **Cohort size** | 100–300 users, deliberately small |
| **Cohort selection** | Mix of: beginners (band 4–5), intermediate (5.5–6.5), advanced (7+); mix of Academic/General; mix of devices |
| **Expectation setting** | "You're an early co-founder. You'll see rough edges. You'll shape the product." |
| **Incentive** | Beta badge on profile (forever), early access to future premium features, direct line to the founder in Discord |
| **Exit criteria** | ≥ 60% of beta users complete the diagnostic; ≥ 40% complete ≥ 3 daily missions; feedback volume ≥ 1 item per 3 users |

### 2.4 Phase 2 — Open Beta (Public Launch)

**Goal:** Maximum adoption of a product we're proud of.

| Gate | Requirement before opening |
|---|---|
| **Reliability** | Uptime ≥ 99.5% for 14 consecutive days in closed beta |
| **Performance** | Page load ≤ 2.5s on 4G; assessment results ≤ 60s median |
| **AI Quality** | Writing/speaking feedback rated ≥ 4/5 in closed beta |
| **Core bugs** | Zero P0/P1 open bugs |
| **Support** | Community mods trained; bug-reporting flow tested |
| **Onboarding** | First-run activation rate ≥ 50% in closed beta |

### 2.5 Launch Day Checklist

- [ ] Landing page updated: "Meet your free AI IELTS mentor — no credit card required."
- [ ] Waitlist users notified with personalized "your mentor is ready" email.
- [ ] Announcement posts: Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit (r/IELTS, r/languagelearning), Indie Hackers.

## 3. User Acquisition

**Principle:** We win with *content, community, and word-of-mouth* — not paid ads (we're not monetizing, so paid CAC is not sustainable).

### 3.1 Channel Strategy Matrix

| Channel | Effort | Expected Impact | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SEO / Content marketing** | High | Medium-long term, compounding | "Band 7 writing structure," "IELTS speaking part 2 framework" — every page a funnel |
| **YouTube (founder-led)** | Medium | High | "I used an AI mentor to prepare for IELTS" — screenshots, before/after, real band results |
| **Reddit & Quora** | Medium | High | Genuinely helpful answers + honest mentions. No astroturfing — it backfires |
| **TikTok / Reels / Shorts** | Medium | High (younger students) | 30–60s "AI corrected my essay in 30 seconds" clips |
| **Facebook / Telegram groups** | Low | Medium | Where serious candidates gather; share free resources, not ads |
| **Study-abroad agencies** | Low | Medium | Offer a free tool their students can use; agencies look generous, we get users |
| **Universities & student clubs** | Low | Medium | Partner with international-student clubs in India, Vietnam, etc. |
| **Referral program** | Medium | High | "Invite a study partner" — both get a badge/feature; see §3.4 |
| **Product Hunt / Indie Hackers** | Low | High (one-time spike) | Launch-day traffic + credibility |

### 3.2 The Activation Funnel

The product itself must convert visitors to active students:

```
Visit landing → Sign up (goal: ≥ 40%)
Sign up → Complete onboarding (goal: ≥ 70%)
Onboarding → Start diagnostic (goal: ≥ 85%)
Diagnostic → Finish diagnostic (goal: ≥ 60%)
Diagnostic → Generate roadmap (goal: ≥ 90%)
Roadmap → Complete first daily mission (goal: ≥ 70%)
Complete 3 missions in week 1 (goal: ≥ 50%)
```

Every step in this funnel is instrumented. Any step below target becomes the focus of the next iteration sprint. **The diagnostic is the conversion moment** — it delivers instant, personalized value ("here is exactly where you are and exactly what to do"), which is the single strongest "aha" moment we have.

### 3.3 Content Engine (Free Resources as Funnels)

We publish genuinely useful free content that demonstrates the product:

| Content Type | Example | Funnel |
|---|---|---|
| **Band 9 sample essays** (AI-generated + human-reviewed) | "Band 9 Opinion Essay: Remote Work" | → Try writing feedback free |
| **Speaking mock transcripts** | "Full Speaking Part 1–3 mock with examiner feedback" | → Try speaking practice |
| **Vocabulary lists** | "Academic Word List — 200 words with examples" | → Interactive vocab builder |
| **Study plan templates** | "8-week plan for Band 6.5 → 7.5" | → Get your personalized roadmap |
| **Diagnostic sample reports** | Anonymized real user reports (with consent) | → Take your diagnostic |
| **YouTube videos** | Screen-recorded walkthroughs of the AI feedback | → Sign up and experience it |

### 3.4 Referral Program (Free-to-Free)

Since nothing is monetized, referrals earn **status, features, and community perks**:

| Referral Action | Reward (both parties) |
|---|---|
| Invite a friend who completes a diagnostic | "Early Adopter" badge + 1 streak freeze |
| Friend completes 3 daily missions | "Study Buddy" badge + custom avatar frame |
| 5 friends complete diagnostic | "Community Builder" role on Discord + priority feature voting |
| 20 friends complete diagnostic | Founder shout-out + lifetime beta badge |

The referral loop is gated on **real engagement** (not just signups) to prevent spam and fake accounts.

### 3.5 Acquisition Do's & Don'ts

| Do ✅ | Don't ❌ |
|---|---|
| Be radically helpful in public communities | Astroturf / fake reviews |
| Share real user stories (with consent) | Overpromise ("guaranteed Band 9") |
| Build in public (post progress, lessons) | Hide limitations |
| Collaborate with IELTS YouTubers (free access) | Pay for placements before product-market fit |
| Answer every comment, DM, and email | Ignore negative feedback — respond publicly |

## 4. Collecting Feedback

**Philosophy:** Feedback is not a support burden — it is our R&D pipeline. Every comment is a product requirement in waiting.

### 4.1 Feedback Channels (In-Product)

| Channel | Placement | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Micro-feedback widget** | Persistent bottom-right; also post-assessment | Rate your experience (1–5) + optional comment |
| **Milestone surveys** | After diagnostic, after 1st week, after 1st mock | Journey-specific sentiment |
| **Exit survey** | When a user churns (7 days inactive) | Why did you leave? (soft, 3 questions max) |
| **NPS survey** | Every 30 days for active users | Net Promoter Score tracking |
| **In-app changelog** | "What's new this week" banner/modal | Users see we're listening → more feedback |

### 4.2 Survey Design Rules

- **Max 3–5 questions** per survey. Long surveys kill response rates.
- **One open-text field always** — the richest signal.
- **Time it right**: survey in the moment (post-diagnostic) beats surveying later.
- **Follow up qualitatively**: users who leave detailed feedback get invited to a 15-minute video interview (offer a free roadmap review as thanks).

### 4.3 Qualitative Interviews (Weekly)

| Who | How many | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| New users (day 1–7) | 2–3 / week | Onboarding friction, aha moments, confusion |
| Users who churned | 1–2 / week | Why they left; what would bring them back |
| Power users (≥ 20 missions) | 1–2 / week | What to deepen; superfan behaviors |
| IELTS tutors (paid advisors) | 1 / month | AI feedback quality, pedagogical gaps |

Every interview is recorded, summarized into 3–5 "insight bullets," and posted to the team's feedback board.

### 4.4 Feedback Triage Rules

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 passionate complaint | Investigate, respond within 24h, fix if valid |
| 3+ similar complaints | Escalate to a sprint item this week |
| Repeated praise of a hack/workaround | Turn the hack into a feature |
| Confusion on a screen | Watch session recordings (with consent); redesign |
| Feature request + 10 upvotes | Add to public roadmap for voting |

## 5. Measuring Success

### 5.1 North Star & Guardrail Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target (90 days post-launch) |
|---|---|---|
| **North Star: Students who reached target band** | Users whose predicted/latest band ≥ target band | 50+ users (phase 1 of proof) |
| **Guardrail: 30-day retention** | % of activated users active on day 30 | ≥ 25% |
| **Guardrail: Churn** | % of users inactive 14+ days | ≤ 8% / month (measured, not a hard gate) |

### 5.2 Core KPI Dashboard

| Category | Metric | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Acquisition** | Signups / week | 500+ by week 8 |
| | Signup → activation (completed 1st mission) | ≥ 40% |
| **Activation** | Diagnostic completion rate | ≥ 60% |
| | Roadmap generation rate | ≥ 90% post-diagnostic |
| **Engagement** | Daily active users (DAU) / weekly active users (WAU) | ≥ 0.25 (Duolingo-class ~0.3) |
| | Missions completed / active user / week | ≥ 3 |
| | Median study minutes / active day | ≥ 20 |
| | Streak retention (users with 7-day streak) | ≥ 15% of activated |
| **Learning outcomes** | Avg band improvement over 30 days (predicted) | ≥ +0.5 |
| | Mock test completion rate | ≥ 40% of users in mock phase |
| | AI feedback satisfaction (post-assessment rating) | ≥ 4.2 / 5 |
| **Feedback loop** | Feedback items / 100 active users / week | ≥ 8 |
| | Median time from report → fix shipped | ≤ 14 days |
| **Community** | Discord members | 2,000+ by month 3 |
| | Community DAU (messages/day) | ≥ 200 |
| | Referral conversion (invite → activated) | ≥ 20% |

### 5.3 Funnel & Health Checks (Weekly Review)

Every Monday, the team reviews:

1. **The activation funnel** (from §3.2) — where are we losing users?
2. **Cohort retention curves** (weekly signup cohorts, D1/D7/D30) — is retention improving with each ship?
3. **Feedback board** — what did users ask for, what shipped, what's the sentiment?
4. **AI quality spot-checks** — 10 random assessments reviewed by a tutor each week.
5. **Infrastructure health** — uptime, error rates, assessment latency.

### 5.4 What "Success" Looks Like (Launch Definition of Done)

At the 90-day mark, the launch is a success if:

- [ ] 2,500+ activated users
- [ ] 30-day retention ≥ 25%
- [ ] ≥ 50 users demonstrably improved band (predicted or mock)
- [ ] ≥ 1,000 pieces of feedback collected and triaged
- [ ] ≥ 25 feedback-driven releases shipped
- [ ] Active community (Discord) with 2,000+ members
- [ ] Monetization decision informed by data (not desperation)

## 6. Bug Reporting

### 6.1 In-App Bug Reporter

| Element | Design |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Shake gesture (mobile) / keyboard shortcut (desktop) / "Report a problem" in footer & help menu |
| **Auto-captured** | Device, OS, browser, screen size, page URL, console errors, network log (last 60s), timestamp |
| **User-provided** | Description (pre-filled template), severity (blocker / annoying / minor), optional screenshot |
| **Submission** | Creates an issue in our tracker (GitHub Issues) with a `bug` label and auto-attached telemetry |

### 6.2 Severity & SLA

| Severity | Definition | Response SLA | Fix SLA |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P0 — Blocker** | Prevents all users from a core action (login, diagnostic, submit) | 1 hour | 24 hours (hotfix) |
| **P1 — Critical** | Prevents a subset of users from a core action; data loss | 4 hours | 3 days |
| **P2 — Major** | Core feature degraded but usable; wrong feedback shown | 24 hours | 1 week |
| **P3 — Minor** | Cosmetic, edge case, non-blocking | 3 days | Next sprint or backlog |
| **P4 — Trivial** | Polish, typo, negligible impact | 1 week | Backlog |

### 6.3 Bug Lifecycle

```
Reported (auto-triage by severity)
  → Triaged (team daily standup; confirm/assign)
  → In Progress
  → Fixed (PR + tests)
  → Verified (staging by QA + reporter notified)
  → Shipped (changelog entry + in-app "what's new" note)
  → Closed (reporter can confirm/close)
```

### 6.4 Bug Report Incentives

- Every verified bug report = **"Bug Hunter" badge** + 25 XP (gamification-consistent).
- P0/P1 reporter = direct thank-you from founder + Discord shout-out.
- Monthly "most valuable bug" = featured on community wall.

## 7. Feature Request System

### 7.1 In-App Request Flow

| Element | Design |
|---|---|
| **Entry** | "Request a feature" in help menu; post-feedback-survey if user rates < 4 |
| **Form** | One-line title, optional detail, category (learning, AI quality, UX, community, performance) |
| **Deduplication** | Auto-suggest similar existing requests ("did you mean: ___?") |
| **Voting** | Upvote existing requests; users see total votes & status |

### 7.2 Public Roadmap

A **public roadmap** (Canny-style, or a simple GitHub Discussions board) shows every request with status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 💡 New | Submitted, awaiting triage |
| 🔍 Investigating | Team evaluating feasibility/impact |
| 📋 Planned | On the roadmap, scheduled |
| 🛠 In Progress | Being built now |
| ✅ Shipped | Released (linked to changelog) |
| 🙅 Won't Do | Not planned — with a reason |

**Why public?** It builds trust, reduces duplicate requests, and makes users feel ownership. We ship in the open.

### 7.3 Triage Framework (Scored 0–100)

```
Priority Score = 0.35 × User Impact (votes × recency)
               + 0.25 × Strategic Fit (free-first mission, core loop)
               + 0.20 × Feasibility (1/engineering effort)
               + 0.20 × Retention Potential (would this keep users coming back?)
```

The top-scored items each week enter the backlog. Every shipped request is credited to the requesters in the changelog ("Built from requests by @user1, @user2, @user3").

### 7.4 Feedback → Requirement Pipeline

```
Raw feedback → Cluster by theme (weekly) → Write insight ("users struggle with X")
  → Propose 1-line requirement → Score & prioritize → Sprint item
  → Ship → Close loop: notify requesters ("You asked, we shipped ✅")
```

Closing the loop is **mandatory** — it is what converts users into advocates.

## 9. Roadmap After Launch

### 9.1 Phase A — Stabilize & Validate (Weeks 1–4)

| Focus | Actions | Success Signal |
|---|---|---|
| **Reliability** | Fix all P0/P1; reduce assessment latency; scale backend | 99.5% uptime; zero P0 open |
| **Funnel** | Instrument every step; fix the weakest step | Activation ≥ 40% |
| **AI quality** | Weekly tutor spot-checks; tune prompts | Feedback rating ≥ 4.2/5 |
| **Feedback loop** | Stand up weekly triage; ship weekly | ≥ 25 feedback items/week |

### 9.2 Phase B — Grow the Core Loop (Months 2–3)

| Focus | Actions | Success Signal |
|---|---|---|
| **Retention** | Streak engine polish; daily-mission UX; notification nudges | 30-day retention ≥ 25% |
| **Referrals** | Launch referral program; optimize invite flow | Referral conversion ≥ 20% |
| **Content engine** | 8+ SEO articles, 4+ YouTube videos | Organic signups ≥ 30% of total |
| **Community** | Discord rituals live; first ambassadors recruited | 1,000+ Discord members |

### 9.3 Phase C — Expand Product Surface (Months 4–6)

| Focus | Actions | Success Signal |
|---|---|---|
| **Full mock tests** | Reading & Listening sections (currently writing/speaking-first) | Mock completion ≥ 40% |
| **Speaking transcription** | Real STT pipeline for speaking feedback | Speaking engagement doubles |
| **AI Brain rollout** | Predicted band, readiness score, risk alerts live | 50+ users hit target band |
| **Mobile experience** | PWA polish; app-store consideration | Mobile retention ≥ desktop |
| **Localization** | UI translations: Hindi, Vietnamese, Spanish | Non-English signups ≥ 25% |

### 9.4 Phase D — Monetization Exploration (After Month 6, Only If Data Supports It)

The decision to monetize is **gated on proof**, never on cash-flow pressure:

| Gate | Threshold |
|---|---|
| 30-day retention | ≥ 30% |
| Organic referral rate | ≥ 25% of signups |
| Love signal | NPS ≥ 50 |
| Outcome proof | 100+ students hit target band |

If gates are met, monetization would start with **non-destructive premium** (per 25_PREMIUM_FEATURES.md): unlimited essays, priority processing, advanced analytics, cosmetics — while the **core mentorship loop stays free forever**. If gates are not met, we keep iterating for free until they are.

### 9.5 Iteration Cadence (The Engine)

```
Every Week:
  Mon — Review KPIs + cohort curves; triage feedback; select sprint items
  Tue–Thu — Build & ship (small, focused batches)
  Fri — Ship-It post; community update; measure Monday's KPIs
Every Month:
  Deep-dive retention analysis; tutor audit of AI quality; strategy reset
```

## 10. Risk Management

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AI feedback quality inconsistent** | Medium | High | Human tutor audit loop; confidence labeling; "provisional" tags on low-confidence scores |
| **Scaling costs outpace users** (free AI is expensive) | Medium | High | Model tiering, caching, rate limits; monitor cost/user weekly; set hard budget cap |
| **Abuse / spam accounts** | Medium | Medium | Email verification, rate limits, anti-cheat (per GAMIFICATION.md §13) |
| **Comparable free tools appear** | Medium | Medium | Community moat, iteration speed, free-first trust — out-execute, don't out-spend |
| **Users churn before "aha" moment** | Medium | High | Ruthless funnel optimization; shorten time-to-first-value (diagnostic) |
| **Pedagogical claims challenged** | Low | High | Always honest ("estimate," not "guarantee"); disclaimers; no fake band promises |
| **Founder burnout (solo/small team)** | Medium | Medium | Scope discipline: ship small weekly; community shares load; automate ops |

## 11. Budget & Cost Guardrails (Free Product)

| Cost Center | Guardrail | Action if Exceeded |
|---|---|---|
| **AI inference (OpenAI/etc.)** | Hard cap per user/day; total monthly cap | Model tiering (mini→full only for complex), cache, batch |
| **Hosting (Vercel/Railway/Render/Supabase)** | Monitor; scale serverless-first | Optimize queries; add caching |
| **Support time** | Community self-serve first | Empower mods; knowledge base |
| **Marketing spend** | $0 paid ads during launch | Content + community only |

The free product must be **sustainable by design**. If AI costs threaten sustainability, we ship **smarter** (caching, model tiering, rate limits) before we ever consider charging.

## 12. Conclusion — What Winning Looks Like

We win when a student who could never afford a $1,000 coaching course opens IELTS AI Coach, takes a diagnostic, and thinks:

> *"This actually understands me. It told me exactly what to do today. It corrected my essay like a real examiner. And it's free. Why would I ever pay for coaching?"*

We win when that student returns **every day for 30 days**, hits their target band, and brings their study partner to the platform.

We win when a **community** of thousands of students around the world are learning together, helping each other, and telling everyone they know about the free AI mentor that replaced expensive coaching.

**We are not launching an app. We are launching a movement to democratize IELTS preparation — and the strategy above is how we win.**
